# Pricing and packaging experiment design

[← Documentation index](README.md) · [Skill](../skills/pricing-experiment/SKILL.md)

A/B tests that optimize clicks but mislead buyers can damage trust.

## Step-by-step founder checklist

1. Inventory real plan limits, taxes, usage and renewal terms.
2. Define primary conversion and guardrail for refunds and support.
3. Test comprehension of plan comparison and cancellation.
4. Keep experiment assignment stable across sessions.
5. Reconcile checkout amount and entitlement to displayed price.
6. Document results, uncertainty and ethics review.

## Test evidence and acceptance

Record build, task, test accounts, environment, expected/actual result, impact, owner and comparable retest. Do not claim a test passed when it has not run. Use synthetic records and authorized environments.
