# Founder pathways — what to test next

[← Resource home](../README.md) · [Learn testing](learning-paths.md) · [Skills](../skills/README.md)

## I have a prototype and no users

Map the intended user and one real task → [neutral research](usability.md) → [accessible forms](forms-validation.md) → [recruit appropriate users](participant-recruitment.md). A demonstration by the founder does not prove task success.

## I have a private beta

[Bug reproduction](qa-automation.md) → [authorization matrix](authentication-authorization.md) → [poor-network mobile checks](low-bandwidth-africa.md) → [monitor errors](performance.md) → [weekly scorecard](quality-metrics.md).

## I am preparing to charge customers

[Subscriptions and billing](saas.md) → [payment reconciliation](payment-reconciliation.md) → [clear cancellation](data-deletion-retention.md) → [support escalation](customer-support-qa.md) → [disaster recovery](disaster-recovery.md).

## I am publishing a native mobile app

[Mobile test matrix](mobile-app-stores.md) → [store reviewer packet](app-store-rejection-recovery.md) → [accessibility](accessibility.md) → [staged release](feature-flags-experiments.md).

## I am launching an AI-powered app or agent

[AI evaluation](ai-evaluation.md) → [agent permission boundaries](ai-agent-permissions.md) → [privacy](security-privacy.md) → [observable production failures](performance.md).

## I am operating a marketplace, Web3 app or civic product

[Marketplace trust](fraud-abuse-trust.md) → [wallet testing](web3-wallet-quality.md) where relevant → [low-bandwidth use](low-bandwidth-africa.md) → [public service inclusivity](public-sector-ngo.md) where relevant.

## After launch

[Incident response](support-operations.md) → [feature flags](feature-flags-experiments.md) → [retest evidence](quality-metrics.md) → [FinOps](finops-cloud-costs.md) → [startup perks](../perks/README.md).

**Every path:** practice one real task, document harm and failure states, prioritize, ship a fix, retest and disclose what remains unverified.
