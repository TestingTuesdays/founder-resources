# Country and industry distribution evidence qualification

[← Distribution](../distribution/README.md) · [Editorial policy](editorial-source-freshness.md)

## Evidence categories

- **Official policy confirmed:** named platform/regulator document, checked date and exact applicability.
- **User research observed:** consented participants, context, sample size and actual task behavior; do not generalize beyond sample.
- **Campaign measured:** actual spend, attribution method, denominator, activation and retention window.
- **Market hypothesis:** plausible channel or format not tested in this market; clearly label as such.
- **Unknown:** payment eligibility, current algorithm, audience prevalence or regulatory obligation not verified.

## Required fields for every country × industry × channel recommendation

Market, buyer segment, product type, channel, original evidence URL, source owner, reviewed date, confidence, local reviewer, policy restrictions, payment merchant status, activation event, campaign results and next review date.

## Promotion gate

A recommendation may move from **hypothesis** to **observed for this segment** only with documented first-party test evidence. A statement that a platform is dominant nationally requires a current, credible country-specific source and methodology; avoid making the claim without it. Official regulator guidance should be checked for the exact regulated activity rather than copied into generic market advice.

## Maintenance

Review source claims before launching a campaign and at material platform/policy changes. Do not imply that all external links have been independently verified merely because the local Markdown paths work. No GitHub Actions are configured.
