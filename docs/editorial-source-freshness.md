# Source freshness and correction policy

[← Home](../README.md) · [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Perks catalog](../perks/README.md)

## Claim evidence levels

1. **Primary source checked:** provider/regulator-owned page or published document, with actual date and scope.
2. **Program discovery:** official program exists but the current offer, deadline or eligibility is not confirmed.
3. **Unverified lead:** plausible resource; do not claim credits, free access, licensing or partnership.
4. **Archived/closed:** historic opportunity retained for context; not currently open.

## Editorial checklist

- Record source URL, checked date, relevant jurisdiction, program tier and exact claim.
- Prefer provider and regulator primary sources over aggregator summaries.
- Distinguish *up to* maximum from typical award; distinguish cloud credits from cash grants.
- Never assert a founder is eligible solely because they operate in Kenya.
- Do not backdate reviews or silently convert unverified leads into verified offers.
- Check deadline, product eligibility, auto-billing and expiry when listing perks.
- Remove or qualify stale price, policy or legal details and link the correction.

## Suggested review cadence

- **Monthly** for time-sensitive perks, application windows and advertised pricing.
- **Before each launch** for payment provider requirements, store rules, tax and regulatory statements.
- **Quarterly** for general documentation and external links, with earlier correction when reported.

These are editorial targets, not automated monitoring commitments. No GitHub Actions are configured.
