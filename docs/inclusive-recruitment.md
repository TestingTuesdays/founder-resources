# Inclusive user research recruiting and incentive design

[← Documentation index](README.md) · [Skill](../skills/inclusive-recruitment/SKILL.md)

A convenience sample can hide critical accessibility and low-bandwidth failures.

## Step-by-step founder checklist

1. Define target personas based on real tasks, not assumptions about demographics.
2. Recruit across representative devices, experience and access needs.
3. Provide clear participation and recording choices.
4. Compensate fairly without pressuring positive responses.
5. Minimize contact and sensitive participant data.
6. Separate findings by observed context, not stereotypes.

## Test evidence and acceptance

Record build, task, test accounts, environment, expected/actual result, impact, owner and comparable retest. Do not claim a test passed when it has not run. Use synthetic records and authorized environments.
