# Structured data and entity markup QA

[← Design and growth hub](README.md) · [Audit template](../templates/design-growth-audit.md)

Ensure machine-readable page metadata matches what people can actually see.

## Actionable procedure

1. Choose supported type based on actual page content, not hoped-for rich result.
2. Use accurate Organization, SoftwareApplication, Product or Article only when applicable.
3. Verify URLs, names, prices, offers, author and dates against rendered content.
4. Avoid fabricated AggregateRating, FAQ or review markup.
5. Test syntax with official validators and monitor Search Console warnings.
6. Recheck after localization, pricing or product changes.

## Evidence checklist

Record URL and build, user/task, expected versus actual, desktop/mobile evidence, severity, change owner and comparable retest. Treat ranking, impressions and conversion changes as observed metrics, never guaranteed outcomes.

## References

- [Google structured data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies)
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/)

No fabricated testimonials, unlicensed design copying or misleading search content. Current search and platform requirements need review before publication.
