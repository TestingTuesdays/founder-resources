# Founder testing resources by app type

[← Resource home](../README.md) · [Kenya-specific guides](../kenya/README.md) · [Reusable skills](../skills/README.md)

Start with your product's **actual user task and risk**, then use the appropriate vertical playbook. A product may belong to several categories.

- [Fintech, wallet and lending apps](fintech-wallet.md) — Transactions, KYC, balance integrity, disputes and regulated activities
- [E-commerce and retail apps](ecommerce.md) — Catalog accuracy, checkout, delivery, refunds and consumer trust
- [Healthtech and care apps](healthtech.md) — Patient safety, clinician roles, consent, health-data privacy and medical claims
- [Education and school apps](edtech.md) — Student and child data, low-cost devices, parent/teacher access and learning outcomes
- [Agritech and farmer services](agritech.md) — Seasonal information, location, connectivity, field operations and payment integrity
- [Logistics, transport and delivery](logistics-mobility.md) — GPS quality, routing, driver identity, dispatch, offline proof and incident response
- [Proptech and housing apps](proptech.md) — Listings, leases, property access, rent payments and document protection
- [Food ordering and hospitality apps](food-delivery.md) — Menus, allergies, food safety, delivery status and refunds
- [Travel, events and booking apps](travel-tourism.md) — Availability, time zones, tickets, cancellations and cross-border guests
- [Gig, freelance and two-sided marketplace apps](marketplace-gig.md) — Trust, identity, matching, payouts, moderation and disputes
- [Govtech, public service and civic apps](govtech-civic.md) — Inclusion, accessibility, public data accuracy and low-connectivity workflows
- [Energy, water utility and IoT apps](energy-water-iot.md) — Meter integrity, tamper evidence, billing, field connectivity and physical safety
- [Creator, streaming and media apps](media-creator.md) — Licensing, subscriptions, playback, attribution, moderation and accessibility
- [NGO, donation and fundraising apps](nonprofit-donations.md) — Donor trust, disbursement evidence, restricted funds, receipts and privacy
- [Social, dating and community apps](social-community.md) — Identity, reporting, abuse, consent, child safety and user-generated content

## Select the implementation stack and feedback workflow

Use the [testing frameworks by stack](../stacks/README.md) alongside the app-specific risk playbook, then [collect user feedback and triage it](../research/README.md) into issues with comparable retests. A stack test passing does not replace observing a target user complete the task.
