# Country opportunity archetypes — where to test first

[← Market selection](README.md) · [Country distribution](../distribution/countries/README.md)

**This is not a ranking of most profitable countries.** These are segment-dependent research starting points. Country attractiveness depends on actual buyers, prices, acquisition channels, legal entity, product economics and execution.

| Market | Hypothetical opportunity | Typical friction to research | Founder first test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | Higher-ticket B2B SaaS, developer tools, specialized software | CAC, enterprise security, state tax and competition | Paid design-partner pilot with measurable ROI |
| United Kingdom | English-language B2B and professional services | UK GDPR, procurement, sector rules | UK-localized pricing and buyer interviews |
| Germany | Industrial software, B2B tools, hardware | German localization, procurement, GDPR, conformity | Technical buyer interviews and certification gap |
| Canada | B2B software, education, healthcare workflows | Provincial rules, English/French, procurement | Province-specific buyer and compliance pilot |
| Kenya | Merchant tools, mobile-money workflows, health and logistics | Payment eligibility, ODPC, KRA, collection risk | Local merchant pilot and actual settlement reconciliation |
| Nigeria | Business software, payments and commerce | Currency, collection, regulatory and fraud exposure | Verified paying cohort with local rails |
| Ghana | Mobile commerce, agriculture and SME tools | Merchant onboarding, local support and market size | Partner-assisted SME pilot |
| Uganda | Agriculture, education and local services | Local distribution, payment onboarding, connectivity | Field partner and low-bandwidth user trial |
| Tanzania | Kiswahili-first commerce and services | Localization, regulation, payment rails | Local-language conversion test |
| Rwanda | Focused enterprise and institutional pilots | Smaller segment size and procurement | Paid institutional design partner |
| South Africa | Enterprise software and professional services | Competition, POPIA and sector rules | B2B account cohort with gross margin |
| India | Developer tools, B2B SaaS, scaled consumer workflows | Localization, competition, payments and pricing | Technical self-serve activation by segment |
| Indonesia | Commerce and mobile-first software | Language, local payments, support and regulation | Creator-to-delivered-order pilot |
| Thailand | Travel, hospitality, local services and SaaS | Thai localization, payments, PDPA | Thai-language booking or business pilot |
| Brazil | Commerce, creator products and SaaS | Portuguese, tax, payments and local support | Localized paid conversion and refunds |
| Ethiopia | Agriculture, business and local-language services | Payments, connectivity, foreign entity restrictions | Feasibility and field research before paid scale |

## Evidence gates

- **Discovery only:** plausible sector/country pairing; no profitability claim.
- **Demand observed:** actual qualified buyer interviews and explicit willingness-to-pay evidence.
- **Paid pilot:** real collected revenue and documented acquisition costs.
- **Retained cohort:** repeat customer value, net contribution and operational risk measured.
- **Scale-ready:** repeatable acquisition and feasible legal/operational structure.

Use [the profitability framework](profitability-framework.md) and [country distribution guides](../distribution/countries/README.md). Never label an entire country profitable solely because its GDP or advertising market is large.
