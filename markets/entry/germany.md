## Official research entry points

- **Business registration:** [Germany Trade & Invest and tax/local trade authorities](https://www.gtai.de/en/invest).
- **Tax authority / related guidance:** [Official starting point](https://www.bzst.de/).

These links are discovery entry points, not verification of eligibility, registration fees, rates or deadlines. Verify the exact legal entity and current activity.

## Before entering Germany

1. Define who contracts with customers, where services are performed, where staff work, where IP is owned and where revenue is collected.
2. Compare remote sales, local distributor, subsidiary and branch. Ask local counsel whether a physical presence or registration is triggered.
3. Verify payment onboarding, bank account eligibility, FX settlement, invoicing, refunds and actual cash collection.
4. Review **VAT, establishment, employment, German language, product conformity** with the appropriate local professional.
5. If hiring or contracting, check employer registration, payroll, immigration and independent-contractor classification.
6. If physical goods, check import classification, duties, certification, warranty, returns and field service.
7. Run a narrow lawful customer pilot, then measure collected contribution margin, CAC, payback, churn and operational risk.

## Caution

A US-incorporated company is not automatically exempt from Germany taxes, local business registration or sector licensing. This is a planning checklist, not legal or tax advice. Use [the market scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md) and a qualified local advisor.
