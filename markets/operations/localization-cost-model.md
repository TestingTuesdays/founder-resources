# Localization and country support cost modeling

## Why this matters

A translated landing page is not a localized business.

## Founder procedure

1. Inventory copy, onboarding, legal terms, receipts, product UX, support and accessibility.
2. Test typography, right-to-left scripts where relevant, dates, phone, currency and taxes.
3. Obtain real translation/review/support quotes and update cost model.
4. Test local support coverage and incident escalation in target time zone.
5. Measure conversion and task completion with actual users.
6. Stop scaling when support or legal coverage is inadequate.

## Required evidence

| Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Specific customer segment and country | To define |
| First-party interviews or paid pilot | Not attempted |
| Actual financial/operational source | Not collected |
| Legal or regulatory questions | Unverified |
| Owner and review date | To assign |
| Decision and comparable retest | Pending |

## Decision rules

Record hypotheses separately from observed metrics. Compare like-for-like periods, currencies, buyer segments and costs. Assign an owner to unresolved requirements and do not call a market profitable without contribution and retention evidence.

**Caution:** Do not infer language preference from nationality or claim translation proves cultural suitability.
