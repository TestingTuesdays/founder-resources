# Sales cycle, invoicing and cash collection risk

[← Market operations](README.md) · [Market profitability](../profitability-framework.md) · [Scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md)

## Why this matters

A signed contract may not translate to collected revenue.

## Founder procedure

1. Map lead, qualified meeting, pilot, contract, invoice, acceptance, collection and renewal.
2. Record typical stage duration from actual CRM evidence, not assumed country averages.
3. Validate procurement, payment terms, withholding and FX treatment with advisors.
4. Model unpaid invoices, refunds, chargebacks and working-capital needs.
5. Segment by company size and buyer type, not just geography.
6. Review paid cohorts and cash conversion before expanding.

## Required evidence

| Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Specific customer segment and country | To define |
| First-party interviews or paid pilot | Not attempted |
| Actual financial/operational source | Not collected |
| Legal or regulatory questions | Unverified |
| Owner and review date | To assign |
| Decision and comparable retest | Pending |

## Decision rules

Record hypotheses separately from observed metrics. Compare like-for-like periods, currencies, buyer segments and costs. Assign an owner to unresolved requirements and do not call a market profitable without contribution and retention evidence.

**Caution:** Cash collected and revenue recognized are not interchangeable accounting concepts.
