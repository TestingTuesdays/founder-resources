## Why this matters

Profitability must be adjusted for execution and regulatory constraints.

## Founder procedure

1. Map entity, sector, tax, data, payments, employment, import and advertising questions.
2. Classify each risk by impact, probability, owner and evidence confidence.
3. Identify blocking requirements versus uncertainties that can be piloted safely.
4. Model downside scenarios for FX, fraud, churn, supply and payment delays.
5. Define trigger and mitigation before spending or signing contracts.
6. Recheck official sources and advisor opinions before go-live.

## Required evidence

| Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Specific customer segment and country | To define |
| First-party interviews or paid pilot | Not attempted |
| Actual financial/operational source | Not collected |
| Legal or regulatory questions | Unverified |
| Owner and review date | To assign |
| Decision and comparable retest | Pending |

## Decision rules

Record hypotheses separately from observed metrics. Compare like-for-like periods, currencies, buyer segments and costs. Assign an owner to unresolved requirements and do not call a market profitable without contribution and retention evidence.

**Caution:** A numeric risk score is a prioritization aid, not a legal approval or measured probability.
