# Market exit, pivot and shutdown planning

[← Market operations](README.md) · [Market profitability](../profitability-framework.md) · [Scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md)

## Why this matters

A reversible pilot can prevent sunk-cost escalation.

## Founder procedure

1. Define pre-launch stop conditions tied to contribution, retention, legal feasibility and harm.
2. Limit fixed commitments and maintain a record of subscriptions, vendors and licenses.
3. Plan refunds, customer export, deletion and contractual notice obligations.
4. Reconcile outstanding receivables, inventory, taxes and employee obligations.
5. Communicate truthfully and retain required accounting records.
6. Document learning and decide pivot, pause, partner or exit.

## Required evidence

| Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Specific customer segment and country | To define |
| First-party interviews or paid pilot | Not attempted |
| Actual financial/operational source | Not collected |
| Legal or regulatory questions | Unverified |
| Owner and review date | To assign |
| Decision and comparable retest | Pending |

## Decision rules

Record hypotheses separately from observed metrics. Compare like-for-like periods, currencies, buyer segments and costs. Assign an owner to unresolved requirements and do not call a market profitable without contribution and retention evidence.

**Caution:** Do not shut down regulated services without qualified advice on customer funds, continuity and retention.
