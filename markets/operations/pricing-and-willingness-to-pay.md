# Pricing and willingness-to-pay research

[← Market operations](README.md) · [Market profitability](../profitability-framework.md) · [Scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md)

## Why this matters

Avoid hypothetical survey pricing as proof of actual payment.

## Founder procedure

1. Interview current alternatives and budgets without anchoring with your preferred price.
2. Test a truthful offer with explicit price, terms and consent.
3. Record completed purchases or signed pilots separately from stated intent.
4. Model discounts, VAT/sales tax applicability, refunds and payment fees.
5. Review willingness to pay by segment, buyer and job rather than country average.
6. Revisit pricing after retained usage and support cost are observed.

## Required evidence

| Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Specific customer segment and country | To define |
| First-party interviews or paid pilot | Not attempted |
| Actual financial/operational source | Not collected |
| Legal or regulatory questions | Unverified |
| Owner and review date | To assign |
| Decision and comparable retest | Pending |

## Decision rules

Record hypotheses separately from observed metrics. Compare like-for-like periods, currencies, buyer segments and costs. Assign an owner to unresolved requirements and do not call a market profitable without contribution and retention evidence.

**Caution:** Pricing experiments must not use deceptive scarcity, hidden fees or unauthorized card charges.
