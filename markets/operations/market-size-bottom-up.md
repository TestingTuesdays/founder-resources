# Bottom-up market sizing and accessible demand

[← Market operations](README.md) · [Market profitability](../profitability-framework.md) · [Scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md)

## Why this matters

TAM is not a sales forecast.

## Founder procedure

1. Define a precise buyer and annual spend for the exact use case.
2. Count reachable accounts from documented sources and separate addressable from serviceable.
3. Exclude customers blocked by procurement, regulations, payment rails or unsupported languages.
4. Estimate conversion using observed pilot evidence; use scenarios when evidence is absent.
5. Compare to top-down estimates as a sanity check without forcing a match.
6. Report range, date, methodology and uncertainty.

## Required evidence

| Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Specific customer segment and country | To define |
| First-party interviews or paid pilot | Not attempted |
| Actual financial/operational source | Not collected |
| Legal or regulatory questions | Unverified |
| Owner and review date | To assign |
| Decision and comparable retest | Pending |

## Decision rules

Record hypotheses separately from observed metrics. Compare like-for-like periods, currencies, buyer segments and costs. Assign an owner to unresolved requirements and do not call a market profitable without contribution and retention evidence.

**Caution:** Never multiply total population by an arbitrary subscription price and call it attainable revenue.
