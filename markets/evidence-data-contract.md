## Separate observations from assumptions

A source-backed market claim needs country, sector, buyer segment, metric definition, units, period, original URL, checked date, publisher, method, confidence and owner. For a founder's own cohort, add product build, acquisition source, sample denominator, actual collected revenue, cost allocation and retention window.

**Evidence types:** official regulatory guidance; published statistical data; direct interview; actual paid pilot; actual retained cohort; model assumption. Do not treat official macroeconomic data as a verified CAC, profit margin or demand estimate for a startup.

## Quality gates

- Every quantitative claim has a unit, period, geography, source and denominator where applicable.
- Different currencies and reporting periods are not ranked directly.
- Tax, sector rules and merchant eligibility require activity-specific primary review.
- Unverified assumptions remain labeled hypothesis; blanks are not silently replaced with zero.
- Corrections preserve the earlier claim, replacement, source and review date.
- No participant identities, contract terms or customer data in public CSVs.

The sample CSV contains **no real market measurements**. [Market interview script](../templates/market-interview-script.md) and [risk register](../templates/market-entry-risk-register.md) help founders collect better evidence.
