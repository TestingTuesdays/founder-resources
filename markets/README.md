# Profitable market selection and how to enter a market

[← Founder resources](../README.md) · [Country distribution](../distribution/countries/README.md) · [Industry distribution](../distribution/industries/README.md)

**There is no universally most profitable country.** A profitable market for a product is the combination of reachable buyers, willingness to pay, contribution margin, acquisition costs, retention, competition, collection risk, tax and regulatory friction. A high-income country can be a poor market for a high-CAC, low-retention product.

## Practical decision

For a US-incorporated B2B SaaS founder, testing US and UK enterprise demand may yield higher contract values but greater sales and procurement costs. For a Kenya-focused merchant fintech, local distribution and payment relevance may be stronger but compliance, collections and support are decisive. For a hardware company, certification, logistics, import duties, serviceability and working capital can dominate software margins. **All examples are hypotheses, not verified rankings.**

Never rank countries by GDP or average income alone. Do not present the examples as current market-size, profit or CAC estimates.
