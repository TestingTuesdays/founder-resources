# Market profitability by industry

[← Market selection](README.md) · [Industry distribution](../distribution/industries/README.md)

| Industry | Main revenue model | Key variable costs | What makes a market attractive | Hard stop / validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B2B SaaS | Seat, usage or contract | Cloud, support, sales servicing | Contract value and retained accounts | Procurement, churn and data requirements |
| AI agents | Task, usage or subscription | Tokens, tools, inference, support | Verified labor/time savings and repeat tasks | Unsafe automation, negative task margin |
| Fintech | Transaction fee, subscription, lending economics | PSP, fraud, compliance, losses | Reliable collections and high repeat utility | Licensing, money movement, default risk |
| Healthtech | B2B provider contract, subscription | Clinical review, support, security | Validated patient/provider outcome and payment | Clinical safety and privacy obligations |
| Edtech | Institution, parent or learner fees | Content, support, distribution | Measurable learning and low churn | Children privacy and school procurement |
| E-commerce | Product gross margin | COGS, shipping, returns, ads | Repeat orders with positive net margin | Refund/fulfillment losses |
| Marketplace | Take rate or subscription | Trust, disputes, payment, incentives | Liquidity and repeat matching | Low fill rate, subsidy dependency |
| Agritech | Service fee, input margin, contract | Field agents, logistics, seasonality | Verified farm value and collections | Unsafe advice, low repeat cash flow |
| Hardware/IoT | Device margin plus service | BOM, tooling, shipping, warranty | Service attach and long-lived installs | Certification, cash cycle, field failures |
| Energy/water | Usage, installation or software | Metering, field service, collections | Predictable utility demand and low losses | Licenses, tariffs, safety, delinquency |
| Creator/media | Advertising, subscription, licensing | Content production, royalties, churn | Owned audience and retained subscribers | Platform dependency, rights |
| Travel/events | Booking/ticket fee | Refunds, chargebacks, service | High conversion and repeat bookings | Double booking and cancellation losses |
| Logistics | Delivery fee, SaaS, brokerage | Drivers, fuel, insurance, support | Dense routes and repeat shipments | Low route density and safety costs |
| Nonprofit/civic | Grants, contracts, donations | Reporting, field operations, fundraising | Repeat funders and demonstrable outcomes | Restricted funds and procurement rules |

## Sector-specific pilot

Measure *collected* revenue, variable costs, acquisition spend, payback, cohort retention, fraud/refunds, cash conversion and fixed compliance/operational costs. Compare like-for-like customer segments across countries. For regulated industries, get expert approval before testing real money or sensitive data.

No sector is inherently profitable in every country.
