## Economics to model

Bill of materials, landed cost, warranty, installation, maintenance and working capital. Split revenue collected, variable service costs, acquisition spend, local fixed costs and working capital. Keep assumptions distinct from actual cohort data.

## Candidate market comparison

Industrial Germany versus US B2B versus East African field service. These are **buyer-segment hypotheses**, not verified national market rankings. Interview equivalent buyer roles and compare like-for-like collected revenue and retention.

## Proof before expansion

- **Customer outcome:** Installed gross margin, service attach, device uptime, return rate.
- **Regulatory and execution constraints:** Certification, customs, radio approvals, safety and product liability.
- **Failure mode:** Positive BOM margin may disappear after tooling, field failures and inventory.

## Research and experiment

1. Choose two countries and one precisely defined buyer segment per market.
2. Obtain actual quotes for price, payment fees, distribution, fulfillment/support and local compliance where applicable.
3. Model conservative, base and optimistic economics with documented input sources.
4. Test a lawful paid pilot; log collected revenue, cohort retention, refunds, variable costs and CAC.
5. Stop when margin is negative without credible improvement, legal feasibility fails or critical user harm remains.
6. Revisit at the next cohort window; never declare a country profitable based only on addressable population.
