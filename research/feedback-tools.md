# Feedback collection tools — choose by evidence and privacy

[← Research hub](README.md) · [Integration guide](feedback-integration.md)

Tool pricing, limits and country availability change. These are discovery links, not verified startup perks.

| [Microsoft Forms](https://forms.microsoft.com/) | Organizational surveys | Check workspace policy |

## Choose the minimum set

**Solo founder:** one intake form + private issue tracker + simple product events. **Early team:** add moderated research and support triage. **Growing product:** add privacy-safe funnel analytics, feature flags and periodic retests. Avoid installing overlapping SDKs without a defined use case.

## Selection checklist

Assess data storage, consent, masking, export, accessibility, localization, cost after free tier, vendor retention, webhooks/API availability, duplicate handling and ability to delete participant data.
