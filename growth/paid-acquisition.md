# Paid ads, attribution and conversion quality

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/paid-acquisition/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Treat ad spend as an experiment with unit economics, consent and explicit stop rules.

## Founder workflow

1. Choose acquisition goal, target market, permitted category and destination conversion event.
2. Audit tracking/consent, UTMs, event deduplication and platform conversion reporting.
3. Define audience, creative variants, spend cap and landing page matching ad promise.
4. Test checkout, signup and post-click activation with synthetic traffic where supported.
5. Monitor CPM, CPC, CTR, CAC, payback and retention by cohort.
6. Stop poor-quality or policy-violating campaigns; separate platform-reported from backend conversions.

## Deliverable

Creative brief, spend guardrails, event map, experiment results and cohort CAC.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

## Validation and ownership

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
