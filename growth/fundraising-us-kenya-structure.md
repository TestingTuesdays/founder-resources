# Fundraising through a US company with Kenyan operations

[← Growth](README.md) · [Investor directory](../investors/README.md) · [Kenya filing](../kenya/company-filing.md)

Compare Delaware company selling remotely, Delaware parent with Kenyan subsidiary, foreign-company Kenyan branch, and direct Kenyan incorporation. Each has different ownership, IP, employment, banking, tax and investor consequences. None automatically removes Kenyan tax or licensing obligations.

**US review:** federal and state returns, Delaware franchise tax, securities exemptions, equity issuance, foreign subsidiary reporting and IP assignment. **Kenya review:** BRS registration, beneficial ownership, KRA taxes, VAT/eTIMS applicability, withholding, payroll, county permits, transfer pricing, permanent establishment and sector approvals. Confirm where investors subscribe for shares and how capital moves between entities.

Prepare cap table, incorporation and board records, IP agreements, accounts, customer contracts, Kenyan permits and tax records (if applicable) in a private data room. Consult US securities counsel, US CPA and Kenyan company secretary/tax advisor. Do not guess tax rates, exemption thresholds or filing dates.

Sources: [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/), [Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/), [BRS](https://brs.go.ke/), [KRA](https://www.kra.go.ke/), [KenInvest](https://eprocedures.investkenya.go.ke/).
