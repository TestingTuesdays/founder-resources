# Fundraising readiness and investor outreach

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/fundraising-readiness/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Match investor mandate, stage, check size, geography and traction before asking for capital.

## Founder workflow

1. Build company narrative, customer evidence, financial model, cap table and data room.
2. Choose round instrument and dilution scenarios with qualified counsel.
3. Segment investors by current fund mandate, stage, sector, ticket and Kenya eligibility.
4. Research actual portfolio investments and conflicts from primary announcements.
5. Write a tailored, concise outreach with traction and specific ask.
6. Track response, diligence, partner meetings, terms and follow-up without fabricating commitments.

## Deliverable

Fundraising data room checklist, investor-fit sheet, outreach tracker and diligence log.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

## Validation and ownership

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
