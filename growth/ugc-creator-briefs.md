# UGC and creator content operations

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/ugc-creator-briefs/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Use genuine customer and creator experiences with permission and transparent disclosures.

## Founder workflow

1. Define objective, persona, actual product claims, creator deliverables and allowed usage rights.
2. Offer formats such as problem-solution, demonstration, tutorial, comparison and day-in-the-life.
3. Write a brief that preserves creator voice and prohibits fabricated results.
4. Record sponsorship disclosure, music/footage licensing and consent.
5. Collect draft, verify factual claims and privacy, then approve distribution.
6. Track cost per qualified activation, creator retention and downstream cohort quality.

## Deliverable

Creator brief, rights/consent record, script options, approval log and outcome report.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

## Validation and ownership

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
