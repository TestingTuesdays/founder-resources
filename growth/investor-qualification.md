# Investor qualification and Kenya deal evidence

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/investor-qualification/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Distinguish a fund that once invested in Kenya from one actively writing new checks in your sector.

## Founder workflow

1. Collect official fund website and portfolio URL with reviewed date.
2. Record investor type, stage, ticket, sectors, Kenya evidence and current fund status.
3. Find named Kenya portfolio company and dated primary announcement when available.
4. Mark open-to-new-investments and ticket size as unknown unless verified.
5. Score mandate fit, traction fit, geographic fit, intro path and competing portfolio exposure.
6. Update status after outreach and recheck sources before claiming active deployment.

## Deliverable

Evidence-backed investor catalog with qualified/unverified status and next action.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
