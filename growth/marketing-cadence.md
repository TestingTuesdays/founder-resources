# Sustainable marketing cadence

[← Growth](README.md) · [Platform format dataset](../data/social-platform-formats.csv)

Use the CSV as a **starting experiment**, not a platform algorithm rule. A weekly loop: Monday audience research and X insight; Tuesday TikTok demo and user feedback; Wednesday product build log and Shorts; Thursday LinkedIn case study and Instagram carousel; Friday retention review and a source-led explainer. No mandatory weekend posting.

Pipeline: idea → fact-check → script → media rights → production → review → publish → UTM tracking → activation/retention analysis. Measure qualified outcomes and record claims, approvals, owner and publication URLs. Platform recommendations and policies evolve; avoid claims about guaranteed viral hooks, hashtag counts or posting hours.
