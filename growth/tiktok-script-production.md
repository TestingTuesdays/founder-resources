# TikTok short-form scripting and production

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/tiktok-script-production/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Make an evidence-backed hook, story and CTA with a shot list and editing plan.

## Founder workflow

1. Choose a real audience question and a fact-checked claim.
2. Write a first-second hook that matches the actual content rather than clickbait.
3. Build a 20–60 second script with voiceover, b-roll, captions, on-screen proof and scene timing.
4. Design a short CTA relevant to the viewer's problem; avoid invented testimonials.
5. Plan safe music/visual licensing, captions, aspect ratio and localization.
6. Review watch-time retention, completion, qualified clicks and activation rather than views alone.

## Deliverable

Timed script, shot list, captions, platform post copy, sources and measurement plan.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
