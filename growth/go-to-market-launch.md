# Go-to-market launch strategy

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/go-to-market-launch/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Define ICP, use case, positioning, channel hypotheses, offer and launch readiness before scheduling posts.

## Founder workflow

1. Interview target users and record their existing alternatives and willingness to switch.
2. Choose one primary segment and job-to-be-done; write a falsifiable positioning statement.
3. Build a channel-by-channel funnel from reach to activation, retention and referral.
4. Set up landing, signup, onboarding, payment and support tracking with privacy safeguards.
5. Run a small launch pilot and compare activated users, not only impressions.
6. Document hypotheses, cost, owner, guardrails and post-launch cohort review.

## Deliverable

ICP interview notes, positioning matrix, channel experiment plan and measured funnel.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

## Validation and ownership

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
