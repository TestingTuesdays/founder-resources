# Retention, virality and network effects

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/retention-virality-network-effects/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Measure repeat value and referrals separately from raw growth and invite counts.

## Founder workflow

1. Define activation and meaningful retained action for the product's natural frequency.
2. Build signup cohorts and D1/D7/D30 or weekly/monthly retention where appropriate.
3. Track churn, resurrection, WAU/MAU, paid retention and revenue expansion by segment.
4. Measure invitations sent, accepted and activated; compute viral coefficient only with explicit denominators.
5. Distinguish network effects (product improves with participant density) from referral loops.
6. Test marketplace liquidity, match rate, time to value and quality as network grows.

## Deliverable

Cohort table, referral funnel, network-density hypothesis and retention interpretation.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.
