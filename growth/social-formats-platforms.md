# Platform-by-platform content formats and cadence

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/social-formats-platforms/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Choose formats that match user intent, platform affordances and production capacity.

## Founder workflow

1. For X, test concise insight, thread, reply, screenshot demo and founder update.
2. For TikTok, test face-to-camera, UGC, voiceover+b-roll, demo, duet/reaction where licensed.
3. For LinkedIn, test professional case study, carousel/document, founder narrative and technical explanation.
4. For Instagram, test Reel, carousel, Story, highlights and community replies.
5. For YouTube, test searchable tutorial, Shorts and long-form product walkthrough.
6. Create a sustainable calendar with review, repurposing, publishing and measurement; avoid fixed algorithm promises.

## Deliverable

Channel matrix, reusable format briefs, editorial calendar and review cadence.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
