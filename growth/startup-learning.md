# Startup School and founder learning curriculum

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/startup-learning/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Build a source-led curriculum covering idea validation, PMF, growth, finance and management.

## Founder workflow

1. Start with YC Startup School and YC Library for founder fundamentals.
2. Use a16z articles/videos for market, distribution, fundraising and company-building perspectives.
3. Watch primary founder/investor interviews and distinguish opinion from empirical fact.
4. Record each resource's target stage, topic, format, author, source and date.
5. Pair each lesson with one experiment in the founder's own product.
6. Update the library when content is unavailable or materially outdated.

## Deliverable

Topic-indexed learning catalog with source URLs and action assignments.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

## Validation and ownership

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
