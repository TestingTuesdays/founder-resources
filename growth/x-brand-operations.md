# Running an X/Twitter brand account

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/x-brand-operations/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Establish voice, content pillars, response practices, social proof and crisis handling without spam.

## Founder workflow

1. Define audience, public brand voice, visual identity, bio, link and pinned product explanation.
2. Plan educational, demonstration, founder-learning, community and support content pillars.
3. Write concise posts with one idea, context and useful media where available.
4. Use a weekly editorial queue with review for claims, privacy, copyright and tone.
5. Respond to genuine questions, track referral traffic with UTM parameters and escalate support privately.
6. Review impressions, engaged visits, signups and retention; do not assume algorithm boosts or fixed ideal times.

## Deliverable

Weekly calendar, sample posts, moderation rules, UTM convention and attribution review.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.

Record responsible founder, source checked date, experiment cohort, baseline, intended outcome, actual outcome and next decision. Platform algorithms, tax law and investor mandates can change; consult current official sources and qualified local counsel for legal/tax matters.
