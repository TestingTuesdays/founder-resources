# US-incorporated company fundraising and operating in Kenya

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/us-kenya-crossborder/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

A Delaware company, Kenyan subsidiary and Kenyan branch can create different tax, employment, banking and investment consequences.

## Founder workflow

1. Map where the company is incorporated, managed, earns revenue, employs people and owns IP.
2. Separate US federal/state reporting from Kenyan corporate, payroll, VAT and withholding questions.
3. Assess Kenyan subsidiary versus foreign-company branch with local counsel and tax advisor.
4. Check cross-border transfer pricing, intercompany agreements, permanent establishment and beneficial ownership.
5. Confirm investor vehicle preference, securities rules and cross-border fund flows.
6. Record legal opinions, filings, deadlines and owner; do not assume US incorporation avoids Kenyan obligations.

## Deliverable

Entity structure diagram, legal/tax question log, filing calendar and advisor review.

## Common failure modes

- Confusing impressions, followers, applications or fundraising announcements with qualified users or closed funding.
- Repeating generic advice without identifying audience, geography, product stage or actual evidence.
- Copying unverified pricing, tax rates, platform algorithms or investor check sizes from old posts.
- Publishing private participant data, exaggerated claims, undisclosed sponsored content or unsupported compliance statements.
