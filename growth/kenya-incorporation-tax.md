# Kenyan company incorporation, ongoing filings and taxes

[← Growth and founder operations](README.md) · [Reusable skill](../skills/kenya-incorporation-tax/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Keep registration, beneficial ownership, tax and county operations separate and current.

## Founder workflow

1. Choose entity type and use official BRS/eCitizen filing channels.
2. Check director/shareholder, registered office and beneficial ownership filing requirements.
3. Set up KRA tax obligations relevant to actual transactions, including eTIMS when applicable.
4. Check payroll, employer contributions and county permits based on operations.
5. Maintain statutory registers, annual returns, accounting records and changes in directors/ownership.
6. Review tax residence, cross-border payments and withholding with a qualified Kenyan advisor.

## Deliverable

Filing checklist with current primary links, applicability and due dates verified privately.
