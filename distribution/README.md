Use **country × industry × channel** to design a launch plan. Every recommendation is a hypothesis until validated with actual user research, official policy and campaign results.

- [Country-by-country distribution](countries/README.md) — localization, payment eligibility, market-entry and regulatory research.
- [Industry-by-industry distribution](industries/README.md) — sector claims, channels, buyer trust and activation metrics.
- [Content distribution channels](channels/README.md) — SEO, short video, communities, email, partners, events, paid ads, app stores, PR and developer ecosystems.
- [Country–industry opportunity matrix](../data/distribution-market-matrix.csv) — structured example research prompts, not verified market rankings.
- [Distribution experiment template](../templates/distribution-experiment.md) — hypothesis, sources, measurement and stop conditions.
- [Distribution planning skill](../skills/distribution-market-planning/SKILL.md) — repeatable market-entry decision workflow.

## How to combine the playbooks

For a Kenyan health app, read Kenya → Healthtech → Search/short video/partnerships. For an Indian developer tool, read India → B2B SaaS/AI → Developer ecosystem/search. Validate local product fit, actual buyer, consent, sector advertising and payment availability before launch.

**Quality gate:** Do not infer market size, preferred platform, regulatory approval or payment provider availability from this library. Use current primary sources and report actual measured activation and retention.
