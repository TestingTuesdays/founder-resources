# Ghana — founder distribution and market-entry playbook

[← Country directory](README.md) · [Industry distribution](../industries/README.md) · [Experiment template](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md)

## Market hypotheses to validate

- **Language and content:** English and locally relevant languages for target audience.
- **Potential channels:** Local creator education, search, community events, WhatsApp and partner referrals.
- **Product localization:** GHS display, mobile money support, carrier/network coverage.
- **Regulatory research:** Bank of Ghana, Data Protection Commission and sector regulators.

These are research starting points, **not measured platform usage, confirmed payment availability, or proof that a channel will work**. Validate for your actual segment, company entity and product.

## Market-entry sequence

1. Identify a narrow target segment, buying job and realistic local alternative; interview at least a small relevant sample before allocating major spend.
2. Select one search/evergreen channel and one community/creator/partner channel that can reach that segment ethically.
3. Localize landing copy, examples, currency, date/number formats, privacy notice, support and CTA; have a fluent reviewer check sensitive claims.
4. Verify merchant onboarding, payment rails, app-store availability, taxes and regulated-sector requirements for the **actual legal entity**.
5. Run a consent-aware pilot with campaign identifiers and server-verified activation events; use a predefined budget and stop condition.
6. Review acquisition cost, task completion, cohort retention, support load and fraud/complaint signals; adapt before scaling.

## Content format tests

| Format | Example angle | Primary metric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Searchable guide | Answer a genuine local problem and show the product limitation | Qualified organic visits and activation |
| Short video | Show the user task in local context with captions | Qualified visits, activation, retention |
| Community answer | Solve an existing question without spam | Useful replies and relevant signups |
| Partner webinar/demo | Demonstrate task with credible local collaborator | Activated leads and follow-up |
| Lifecycle email/message | Onboard consented users after signup | First value event and opt-outs |

## Required evidence

Record local interview notes (privately), official source URL and review date, translation reviewer, payment eligibility decision, campaign spend, attribution method, conversion denominator, activation, cohort retention and a named owner. Unknown items remain **unverified**.
