# Search and evergreen documentation — content distribution playbook

[← Channel directory](README.md) · [Countries](../countries/README.md) · [Industries](../industries/README.md)

## Channel objective

Search intent, original answers, structured navigation and accurate citations.

## Suitable content and placements

Indexable guides, help center, comparison, tutorials.

## Repeatable execution

1. Define the intended user, relevant problem, product stage and desired user value event.
2. Check the channel's current policies, spam restrictions, paid promotion rules and content rights.
3. Prepare one source-backed original piece and adapt to the format rather than mass-posting duplicates.
4. Publish through authorized brand accounts or consenting partners, with truthful disclosure and attribution.
5. Instrument UTM/referral source and server-verified activation while respecting consent and privacy.
6. Compare quality and retained outcomes, not just reach; document findings and the next iteration.

## Measurement

Qualified organic visit → activation; content freshness. Track denominator, attribution limitations, cohort, spend, and complaints/opt-outs. Do not claim algorithmic distribution guarantees.

## Failure cases

- Campaign message and landing page promise do not match.
- Tracking captures private data or ignores opt-out.
- High impressions or clicks but no completed value event.
- Content is reposted without rights, creator disclosure or required sector review.
- Referral or attribution is double counted across channels.
