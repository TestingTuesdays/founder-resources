# Country launch research and validation

[← Distribution operations](README.md) · [Experiment worksheet](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md)

## Goal

Test local demand before scaling marketing.

## Practical execution

1. Select one country, one city/segment and one buyer task; document why this segment is reachable.
2. Interview potential users and local operators; record their existing alternatives and decision criteria.
3. Review payment provider merchant eligibility for the actual company and relevant advertising/data rules.
4. Audit localized landing, pricing, support and onboarding on representative devices.
5. Run one capped organic/partner pilot and record source, qualified leads and activated users.
6. Review cohorts and complaints before scaling or declaring country-market fit.

## Evidence and stop conditions

| Item | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Segment and country | Actual buyer and market, not broad population |
| Hypothesis | Specific behavior and expected result |
| Source | Official provider/policy source and checked date |
| Creative | Version, language, disclosure and rights |
| Attribution | UTM, server event and consent mechanism |
| Success | Qualified activation and retained value event |
| Guardrails | Complaints, refunds, safety, CAC and privacy |
| Decision | Continue, revise, stop or inconclusive |

Do not invent campaign results or assume platform algorithms, payment access or local licensing. Compare outcomes against the original hypothesis and record the owner.
