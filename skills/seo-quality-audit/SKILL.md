# Search and AI discovery quality audit

[← Skills](../README.md) · [SEO guide](../../docs/seo-discovery.md)

## Inputs

Public routes, target users, intended canonical URLs, sitemap, primary product facts and access to search tooling.

## Procedure

1. Inspect redirect, canonical, robots and HTTP behavior of relevant routes.
2. Check sitemap coverage and compare page titles/descriptions with visible text.
3. Verify structured data using official validators; remove unsupported claims.
4. Check localization alternates and share previews on representative pages.
5. Test mobile content accessibility, performance and coherent navigation.
6. Document findings and retest after publication.

## Done when

Evidence-backed route checklist and fixes exist. Do not promise search rankings, fake third-party endorsements, or hide misleading text for crawlers.
