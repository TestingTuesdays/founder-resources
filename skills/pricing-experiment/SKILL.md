# Pricing and packaging experiment design

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Guide](../../docs/pricing-experiment.md)

## Inputs

Versioned product, real target user task, synthetic test accounts, authorized environment and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Inventory real plan limits, taxes, usage and renewal terms.
2. Define primary conversion and guardrail for refunds and support.
3. Test comprehension of plan comparison and cancellation.
4. Keep experiment assignment stable across sessions.
5. Reconcile checkout amount and entitlement to displayed price.
6. Document results, uncertainty and ethics review.

## Completion criteria

Evidence-backed issue register with observed outcomes, severity, owner, fix, build and retest. Separate hypotheses and proposed tests from executed tests.

## Privacy and safety

Never publish participant identities, payment details, credentials or unapproved security findings. Escalate regulated or safety-critical questions for expert review.
