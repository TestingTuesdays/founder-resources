# Kenya field research, older Android and language testing — reusable skill

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../kenya/integrations/kenya-usability-research.md)

## Inputs

Real business activity description, current official provider/regulator documents, synthetic accounts, approved sandbox and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Recruit participants based on actual task and context rather than assumed demographics.
2. Test English/Kiswahili wording and any other required language with fluent reviewers.
3. Use a representative low-memory Android device and slow/unstable mobile network.
4. Test shared devices, low storage, power interruption and mobile-money prompt switching.
5. Verify phone formats, KES display, location uncertainty and accessible errors.
6. Record unassisted completion, failed tasks and compensation/consent.
7. Retest improvements with matched task and network conditions.

## Done when

A completed scenario matrix documents actual behavior, risk, owner, fix and comparable retest. Record any licensing, merchant onboarding or tax decision as **unverified** until confirmed with the competent source.

## Safety

Never use real customer records, production secrets or real money in open testing. Do not confuse a documented plan with an executed integration.
