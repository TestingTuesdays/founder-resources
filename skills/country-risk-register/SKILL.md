# Country market-entry risk register

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Full procedure](../../markets/operations/country-risk-register.md)

## Inputs

Specific buyer and market, product version, legal entity, financial assumptions, primary sources, test owner and privacy-safe evidence.

## Procedure

1. Map entity, sector, tax, data, payments, employment, import and advertising questions.
2. Classify each risk by impact, probability, owner and evidence confidence.
3. Identify blocking requirements versus uncertainties that can be piloted safely.
4. Model downside scenarios for FX, fraud, churn, supply and payment delays.
5. Define trigger and mitigation before spending or signing contracts.
6. Recheck official sources and advisor opinions before go-live.

## Output

A source-linked decision record with unknowns, observed results, owner, stop conditions and next review. Mark unrun research as not attempted.

## Guardrails

A numeric risk score is a prioritization aid, not a legal approval or measured probability. No invented financial metrics, unauthorized user data or unsupported legal conclusions.
