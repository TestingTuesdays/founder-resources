# Local-language and culturally appropriate creative

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed playbook](../../distribution/operations/local-language-creative.md)

## Inputs

Product, specific user segment, country, industry, legal entity, authorized accounts, source documents, experiment owner and measurement plan.

## Procedure

1. Write source-language message and identify the intended user job.
2. Commission or recruit fluent reviewer for idioms, claims, tone and accessibility.
3. Check fonts, captions, numerals, dates, payment labels and CTA.
4. Test variants with target-language users using neutral comprehension tasks.
5. Keep screenshots, rights and approvals in a private production log.
6. Compare task completion and conversion, not only click-through.

## Output

Complete the [distribution experiment worksheet](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md), linking primary sources and actual outcomes. Label unexecuted tests not attempted.

## Safeguards

Respect privacy, advertising disclosures, content rights, restricted sectors and local regulatory review. No spam or invented metrics.
