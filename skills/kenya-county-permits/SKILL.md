# County permits, field operations and regulated marketplace discovery — reusable skill

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../kenya/integrations/county-permits.md)

## Inputs

Real business activity description, current official provider/regulator documents, synthetic accounts, approved sandbox and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Describe where physical goods/services are provided and by whom.
2. List each county of operations and the specific physical activity.
3. Identify county permits and sector regulator approvals potentially relevant.
4. Confirm whether platform is a directory, seller, intermediary or regulated operator.
5. Document source, current fee/expiry if verified, authority and owner.
6. Avoid making approval claims until confirmed by responsible office or counsel.
7. Retest operational workflows after approval conditions change.

## Done when

A completed scenario matrix documents actual behavior, risk, owner, fix and comparable retest. Record any licensing, merchant onboarding or tax decision as **unverified** until confirmed with the competent source.

## Safety

Never use real customer records, production secrets or real money in open testing. Do not confuse a documented plan with an executed integration.
