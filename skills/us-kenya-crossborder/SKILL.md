# US-incorporated company fundraising and operating in Kenya

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/us-kenya-crossborder.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Map where the company is incorporated, managed, earns revenue, employs people and owns IP.
2. Separate US federal/state reporting from Kenyan corporate, payroll, VAT and withholding questions.
3. Assess Kenyan subsidiary versus foreign-company branch with local counsel and tax advisor.
4. Check cross-border transfer pricing, intercompany agreements, permanent establishment and beneficial ownership.
5. Confirm investor vehicle preference, securities rules and cross-border fund flows.
6. Record legal opinions, filings, deadlines and owner; do not assume US incorporation avoids Kenyan obligations.

## Completion

Deliver entity structure diagram, legal/tax question log, filing calendar and advisor review. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
