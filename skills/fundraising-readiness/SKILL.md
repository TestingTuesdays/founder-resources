# Fundraising readiness and investor outreach

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/fundraising-readiness.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Build company narrative, customer evidence, financial model, cap table and data room.
2. Choose round instrument and dilution scenarios with qualified counsel.
3. Segment investors by current fund mandate, stage, sector, ticket and Kenya eligibility.
4. Research actual portfolio investments and conflicts from primary announcements.
5. Write a tailored, concise outreach with traction and specific ask.
6. Track response, diligence, partner meetings, terms and follow-up without fabricating commitments.

## Completion

Deliver fundraising data room checklist, investor-fit sheet, outreach tracker and diligence log. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
