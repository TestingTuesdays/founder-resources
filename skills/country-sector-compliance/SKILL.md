# Country and sector marketing compliance review

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed playbook](../../distribution/operations/country-sector-compliance.md)

## Inputs

Product, specific user segment, country, industry, legal entity, authorized accounts, source documents, experiment owner and measurement plan.

## Procedure

1. Map product activity, target market, data flow, payment rail and legal entity.
2. Identify current sector regulator and platform restricted-ad categories.
3. Verify claims, sponsorship disclosures, marketing consent and unsubscribe routes.
4. Review child, health, finance, lending, investment and political content safeguards when relevant.
5. Record primary-source URL, exact requirement, jurisdiction, checked date and qualified reviewer.
6. Block launch when required approval or eligibility remains unresolved.

## Output

Complete the [distribution experiment worksheet](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md), linking primary sources and actual outcomes. Label unexecuted tests not attempted.

## Safeguards

Respect privacy, advertising disclosures, content rights, restricted sectors and local regulatory review. No spam or invented metrics.
