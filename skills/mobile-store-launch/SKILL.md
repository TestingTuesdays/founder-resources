# Mobile app-store launch

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Topic guide](../../docs/mobile-app-stores.md)

## Objective

Prepare a trustworthy iOS/Android submission with review-safe behavior.

## Required inputs

Release builds, sandbox/reviewer credentials, screenshots, privacy declarations.

## Procedure

1. Test physical devices, fresh install and upgrades.
2. Check permissions denied and offline/restore paths.
3. Validate account deletion, subscriptions and accessibility where applicable.
4. Review current Apple and Google policies directly.
5. Verify store metadata, ratings, localization and reviewer instructions.
6. Stage rollout and monitor crashes or rejections.

## Done when

Checklist with store-specific evidence and unresolved blockers.

## Safety and evidence

Use permissioned systems and synthetic data. Obtain research consent where applicable, redact personal information, preserve actual vs expected outcomes, and never state a check passed without running it. Human review remains necessary for high-risk conclusions.
