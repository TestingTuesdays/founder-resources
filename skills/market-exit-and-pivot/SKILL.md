# Market exit, pivot and shutdown planning

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Full procedure](../../markets/operations/market-exit-and-pivot.md)

## Inputs

Specific buyer and market, product version, legal entity, financial assumptions, primary sources, test owner and privacy-safe evidence.

## Procedure

1. Define pre-launch stop conditions tied to contribution, retention, legal feasibility and harm.
2. Limit fixed commitments and maintain a record of subscriptions, vendors and licenses.
3. Plan refunds, customer export, deletion and contractual notice obligations.
4. Reconcile outstanding receivables, inventory, taxes and employee obligations.
5. Communicate truthfully and retain required accounting records.
6. Document learning and decide pivot, pause, partner or exit.

## Output

A source-linked decision record with unknowns, observed results, owner, stop conditions and next review. Mark unrun research as not attempted.

## Guardrails

Do not shut down regulated services without qualified advice on customer funds, continuity and retention. No invented financial metrics, unauthorized user data or unsupported legal conclusions.
