# International business entry

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Operating models](../../markets/market-entry-operations.md)

## Inputs

Existing legal entity, proposed country, product activity, payment and data flows, planned hires, expected revenue, local counsel/tax advisor and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Define what the company will sell, to whom, where it will be delivered and who will contract.
2. Compare cross-border sale, distributor, subsidiary, branch and staffing alternatives.
3. Research current incorporation, tax nexus, VAT, withholding, employment, data and sector licensing.
4. Verify local merchant/banking eligibility, customer support and lawful advertising.
5. Calculate contribution margin, landed costs (if physical), local fixed costs and working capital.
6. Pilot demand lawfully, then get qualified sign-off before local filings or regulated transactions.
7. Assign filing owners, source dates, deadlines and ongoing compliance reviews.

## Output

A jurisdiction-specific structure decision, source-backed question log, [market scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md) and local professional review.

## Safety

Do not assert a company is compliant, licensed, tax-exempt or permitted to hire without current official evidence and qualified advice.
