# Recruit and moderate target-user sessions

[← All skills](../README.md) · [Practical guide](../../docs/participant-recruitment.md)

## Goal

Make a founder's testing action repeatable and independently verifiable.

## Required inputs

An authorized product/environment, versioned build, representative sample accounts, explicit pass/fail criterion, and appropriate privacy/research permissions.

## Procedure

1. Define eligibility by actual tasks and behavior without relying on stereotypes.
2. Recruit with consent, compensation/accommodation terms and privacy notice.
3. Prepare a neutral task; do not guide participants to the expected button.
4. Record unassisted completion, confusion and recovery in anonymized notes.
5. Synthesize observations, disclose sample limitations and retest changes.

## Done when

A private roster and consent records plus safe, anonymized founder-facing findings.

## Restrictions

Do not state an outcome as passed until it was run. Use synthetic data for public examples and keep billing identifiers, participants' information, secrets and private accounts out of the open repository. Stop and escalate if the test could harm real customers or physical systems.
