# Inclusive user research recruiting and incentive design

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Guide](../../docs/inclusive-recruitment.md)

## Inputs

Versioned product, real target user task, synthetic test accounts, authorized environment and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Define target personas based on real tasks, not assumptions about demographics.
2. Recruit across representative devices, experience and access needs.
3. Provide clear participation and recording choices.
4. Compensate fairly without pressuring positive responses.
5. Minimize contact and sensitive participant data.
6. Separate findings by observed context, not stereotypes.

## Completion criteria

Evidence-backed issue register with observed outcomes, severity, owner, fix, build and retest. Separate hypotheses and proposed tests from executed tests.

## Privacy and safety

Never publish participant identities, payment details, credentials or unapproved security findings. Escalate regulated or safety-critical questions for expert review.
