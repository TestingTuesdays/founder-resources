# Kenya location pages and legitimate local SEO

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Guide](../../design-growth/local-seo-kenya.md)

## Inputs

Versioned website/app, core user task, audience, relevant source evidence and authorized testing environment.

## Steps

1. Confirm physical address, operating hours, service area and business eligibility.
2. Keep location pages distinct and useful, with actual local details and support.
3. Avoid fabricated branches, review snippets and doorway city pages.
4. Verify Google Business Profile eligibility and consistency where applicable.
5. Test mobile click-to-call, directions, local language and low-bandwidth load.
6. Measure genuine location-specific queries and conversion quality.

## Deliverable

Ranked issues with page, evidence, severity, recommended fix, owner, expected effect, release version and observed retest. Separate hypothetical improvements from measured results.

## Safety

No private data in public issues, no invented rankings, reviews or compliance claims, and no copying of protected design assets.
