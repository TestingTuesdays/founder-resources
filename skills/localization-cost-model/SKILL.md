# Localization and country support cost modeling

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Full procedure](../../markets/operations/localization-cost-model.md)

## Inputs

Specific buyer and market, product version, legal entity, financial assumptions, primary sources, test owner and privacy-safe evidence.

## Procedure

1. Inventory copy, onboarding, legal terms, receipts, product UX, support and accessibility.
2. Test typography, right-to-left scripts where relevant, dates, phone, currency and taxes.
3. Obtain real translation/review/support quotes and update cost model.
4. Test local support coverage and incident escalation in target time zone.
5. Measure conversion and task completion with actual users.
6. Stop scaling when support or legal coverage is inadequate.

## Output

A source-linked decision record with unknowns, observed results, owner, stop conditions and next review. Mark unrun research as not attempted.

## Guardrails

Do not infer language preference from nationality or claim translation proves cultural suitability. No invented financial metrics, unauthorized user data or unsupported legal conclusions.
