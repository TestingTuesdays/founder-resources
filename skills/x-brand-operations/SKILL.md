# Running an X/Twitter brand account

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/x-brand-operations.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Define audience, public brand voice, visual identity, bio, link and pinned product explanation.
2. Plan educational, demonstration, founder-learning, community and support content pillars.
3. Write concise posts with one idea, context and useful media where available.
4. Use a weekly editorial queue with review for claims, privacy, copyright and tone.
5. Respond to genuine questions, track referral traffic with UTM parameters and escalate support privately.
6. Review impressions, engaged visits, signups and retention; do not assume algorithm boosts or fixed ideal times.

## Completion

Deliver weekly calendar, sample posts, moderation rules, utm convention and attribution review. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
