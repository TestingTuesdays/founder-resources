# Trust signals, testimonials and product claims QA

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Guide](../../design-growth/trust-signals-claims.md)

## Inputs

Versioned website/app, core user task, audience, relevant source evidence and authorized testing environment.

## Steps

1. Inventory claims, customer logos, testimonials, certifications and security badges.
2. Request consent/rights and verify source, scope and current accuracy.
3. Remove fabricated reviews, false scarcity and unsupported compliance statements.
4. Check support, pricing, refund and privacy information is easy to find.
5. Review metadata and structured data for consistency with actual claims.
6. Retest whether users understand product limits and next steps.

## Deliverable

Ranked issues with page, evidence, severity, recommended fix, owner, expected effect, release version and observed retest. Separate hypothetical improvements from measured results.

## Safety

No private data in public issues, no invented rankings, reviews or compliance claims, and no copying of protected design assets.
