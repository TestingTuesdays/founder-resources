# Cross-channel attribution and distribution metrics

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed playbook](../../distribution/operations/distribution-measurement.md)

## Inputs

Product, specific user segment, country, industry, legal entity, authorized accounts, source documents, experiment owner and measurement plan.

## Procedure

1. Define source, medium, campaign, creative and content IDs consistently.
2. Instrument server-confirmed signup, activation, purchase and retention separately.
3. Capture attribution consent and avoid personal identifiers in URLs.
4. Test direct traffic, missing UTM, app-store transitions and delayed payments.
5. Reconcile platform-reported conversions with backend events and refunds.
6. Review CAC, payback, retention, complaints and uncertainty by country and channel.

## Output

Complete the [distribution experiment worksheet](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md), linking primary sources and actual outcomes. Label unexecuted tests not attempted.

## Safeguards

Respect privacy, advertising disclosures, content rights, restricted sectors and local regulatory review. No spam or invented metrics.
