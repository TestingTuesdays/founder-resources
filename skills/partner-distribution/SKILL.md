# Partner and reseller distribution

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed playbook](../../distribution/operations/partner-distribution.md)

## Inputs

Product, specific user segment, country, industry, legal entity, authorized accounts, source documents, experiment owner and measurement plan.

## Procedure

1. Define buyer, partner incentive, channel conflict and attribution boundaries.
2. Qualify partner credibility and right to represent product claims.
3. Agree deliverables, referral tracking, privacy, support and disclosures.
4. Run a pilot webinar/demo or referral with capped resources.
5. Reconcile partner-sourced signups to server-verified activation and retained accounts.
6. Document settlement, renewals, complaints and termination rights.

## Output

Complete the [distribution experiment worksheet](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md), linking primary sources and actual outcomes. Label unexecuted tests not attempted.

## Safeguards

Respect privacy, advertising disclosures, content rights, restricted sectors and local regulatory review. No spam or invented metrics.
