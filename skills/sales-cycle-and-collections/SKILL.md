# Sales cycle, invoicing and cash collection risk

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Full procedure](../../markets/operations/sales-cycle-and-collections.md)

## Inputs

Specific buyer and market, product version, legal entity, financial assumptions, primary sources, test owner and privacy-safe evidence.

## Procedure

1. Map lead, qualified meeting, pilot, contract, invoice, acceptance, collection and renewal.
2. Record typical stage duration from actual CRM evidence, not assumed country averages.
3. Validate procurement, payment terms, withholding and FX treatment with advisors.
4. Model unpaid invoices, refunds, chargebacks and working-capital needs.
5. Segment by company size and buyer type, not just geography.
6. Review paid cohorts and cash conversion before expanding.

## Output

A source-linked decision record with unknowns, observed results, owner, stop conditions and next review. Mark unrun research as not attempted.

## Guardrails

Cash collected and revenue recognized are not interchangeable accounting concepts. No invented financial metrics, unauthorized user data or unsupported legal conclusions.
