# Bottom-up market sizing and accessible demand

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Full procedure](../../markets/operations/market-size-bottom-up.md)

## Inputs

Specific buyer and market, product version, legal entity, financial assumptions, primary sources, test owner and privacy-safe evidence.

## Procedure

1. Define a precise buyer and annual spend for the exact use case.
2. Count reachable accounts from documented sources and separate addressable from serviceable.
3. Exclude customers blocked by procurement, regulations, payment rails or unsupported languages.
4. Estimate conversion using observed pilot evidence; use scenarios when evidence is absent.
5. Compare to top-down estimates as a sanity check without forcing a match.
6. Report range, date, methodology and uncertainty.

## Output

A source-linked decision record with unknowns, observed results, owner, stop conditions and next review. Mark unrun research as not attempted.

## Guardrails

Never multiply total population by an arbitrary subscription price and call it attainable revenue. No invented financial metrics, unauthorized user data or unsupported legal conclusions.
