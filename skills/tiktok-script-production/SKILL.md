# TikTok short-form scripting and production

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/tiktok-script-production.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Choose a real audience question and a fact-checked claim.
2. Write a first-second hook that matches the actual content rather than clickbait.
3. Build a 20–60 second script with voiceover, b-roll, captions, on-screen proof and scene timing.
4. Design a short CTA relevant to the viewer's problem; avoid invented testimonials.
5. Plan safe music/visual licensing, captions, aspect ratio and localization.
6. Review watch-time retention, completion, qualified clicks and activation rather than views alone.

## Completion

Deliver timed script, shot list, captions, platform post copy, sources and measurement plan. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
