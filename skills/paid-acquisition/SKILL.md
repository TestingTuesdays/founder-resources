# Paid ads, attribution and conversion quality

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/paid-acquisition.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Choose acquisition goal, target market, permitted category and destination conversion event.
2. Audit tracking/consent, UTMs, event deduplication and platform conversion reporting.
3. Define audience, creative variants, spend cap and landing page matching ad promise.
4. Test checkout, signup and post-click activation with synthetic traffic where supported.
5. Monitor CPM, CPC, CTR, CAC, payback and retention by cohort.
6. Stop poor-quality or policy-violating campaigns; separate platform-reported from backend conversions.

## Completion

Deliver creative brief, spend guardrails, event map, experiment results and cohort cac. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
