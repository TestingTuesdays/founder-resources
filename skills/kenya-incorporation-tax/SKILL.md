# Kenyan company incorporation, ongoing filings and taxes

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/kenya-incorporation-tax.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Choose entity type and use official BRS/eCitizen filing channels.
2. Check director/shareholder, registered office and beneficial ownership filing requirements.
3. Set up KRA tax obligations relevant to actual transactions, including eTIMS when applicable.
4. Check payroll, employer contributions and county permits based on operations.
5. Maintain statutory registers, annual returns, accounting records and changes in directors/ownership.
6. Review tax residence, cross-border payments and withholding with a qualified Kenyan advisor.

## Completion

Deliver filing checklist with current primary links, applicability and due dates verified privately. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
