# Go-to-market launch strategy

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/go-to-market-launch.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Interview target users and record their existing alternatives and willingness to switch.
2. Choose one primary segment and job-to-be-done; write a falsifiable positioning statement.
3. Build a channel-by-channel funnel from reach to activation, retention and referral.
4. Set up landing, signup, onboarding, payment and support tracking with privacy safeguards.
5. Run a small launch pilot and compare activated users, not only impressions.
6. Document hypotheses, cost, owner, guardrails and post-launch cohort review.

## Completion

Deliver icp interview notes, positioning matrix, channel experiment plan and measured funnel. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
