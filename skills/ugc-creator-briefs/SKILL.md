# UGC and creator content operations

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/ugc-creator-briefs.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Define objective, persona, actual product claims, creator deliverables and allowed usage rights.
2. Offer formats such as problem-solution, demonstration, tutorial, comparison and day-in-the-life.
3. Write a brief that preserves creator voice and prohibits fabricated results.
4. Record sponsorship disclosure, music/footage licensing and consent.
5. Collect draft, verify factual claims and privacy, then approve distribution.
6. Track cost per qualified activation, creator retention and downstream cohort quality.

## Completion

Deliver creator brief, rights/consent record, script options, approval log and outcome report. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
