# Kenyan payment provider evaluation and selection — reusable skill

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../kenya/integrations/psp-selection.md)

## Inputs

Real business activity description, current official provider/regulator documents, synthetic accounts, approved sandbox and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Define legal incorporation, business category and target settlement currency.
2. Confirm provider merchant onboarding for Kenya and permitted business model.
3. Compare M-Pesa, card, bank and international rails available to your account.
4. Request written current fee, chargeback, payout schedule and reserve details.
5. Review hosted checkout versus direct integration, fraud, webhook signing and test environment.
6. Test refunds, reversals, partial settlements and support escalation.
7. Calculate true total cost including FX, tax, dispute fees and delayed payouts.

## Done when

A completed scenario matrix documents actual behavior, risk, owner, fix and comparable retest. Record any licensing, merchant onboarding or tax decision as **unverified** until confirmed with the competent source.

## Safety

Never use real customer records, production secrets or real money in open testing. Do not confuse a documented plan with an executed integration.
