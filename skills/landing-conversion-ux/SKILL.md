# Landing page clarity and ethical conversion — reusable skill

[← Skill library](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../design-growth/landing-conversion-ux.md)

## Inputs

Live or staging URL, target audience, core user journey, representative devices, current product facts and authorized testing access.

## Procedure

1. Identify target persona, promise, proof and one primary CTA.
2. Show who the product is for, what it does, and real product evidence above or near the fold.
3. Audit form length, error recovery, pricing clarity, trust claims and cancellation.
4. Check mobile hero, sticky nav, layout shift and loading performance.
5. Test CTA labels with users, not only click counts.
6. Use consent-aware analytics and distinguish acquisition, activation and retained success.

## Output

A prioritized audit: URL/component, problem, impact, evidence, recommended change, owner, expected outcome, verification status and comparable retest.

## Rules

No guaranteed rankings, fabricated testimonials, hidden SEO manipulation or unlicensed copied assets. Distinguish research from tested results. Use the [audit template](../../templates/design-growth-audit.md).
