# ODPC-ready personal-data inventory and vendor map — reusable skill

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../kenya/integrations/odpc-data-map.md)

## Inputs

Real business activity description, current official provider/regulator documents, synthetic accounts, approved sandbox and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. List each field collected, why, lawful basis to verify and user population.
2. Identify controllers/processors, cloud regions, third parties and transfer destinations.
3. Classify sensitive records, children and any high-risk automated decisions.
4. Record retention and deletion behavior including backups and analytics.
5. Test subject access, correction, withdrawal and deletion with synthetic accounts.
6. Review whether registration, DPIA or notification requirements apply to the business.
7. Have qualified local reviewer sign off unresolved obligations.

## Done when

A completed scenario matrix documents actual behavior, risk, owner, fix and comparable retest. Record any licensing, merchant onboarding or tax decision as **unverified** until confirmed with the competent source.

## Safety

Never use real customer records, production secrets or real money in open testing. Do not confuse a documented plan with an executed integration.
