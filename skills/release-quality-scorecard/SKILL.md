# Produce an evidence-based founder QA scorecard

[← All skills](../README.md) · [Practical guide](../../docs/quality-metrics.md)

## Goal

Make a founder's testing action repeatable and independently verifiable.

## Required inputs

An authorized product/environment, versioned build, representative sample accounts, explicit pass/fail criterion, and appropriate privacy/research permissions.

## Procedure

1. List critical tasks and expected outcomes before the session.
2. Record planned, attempted, unattempted and failed tests separately.
3. Categorize P0/P1 issues and attach reproducible evidence without secrets.
4. Link fix to deployed build/version and retest original task.
5. Present trends and uncertainty rather than claiming unearned confidence.

## Done when

Weekly task-success, open-risk and retest summary with denominators and honest limitations.

## Restrictions

Do not state an outcome as passed until it was run. Use synthetic data for public examples and keep billing identifiers, participants' information, secrets and private accounts out of the open repository. Stop and escalate if the test could harm real customers or physical systems.
