# Creator and UGC distribution across markets

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed playbook](../../distribution/operations/creator-distribution.md)

## Inputs

Product, specific user segment, country, industry, legal entity, authorized accounts, source documents, experiment owner and measurement plan.

## Procedure

1. Choose creators based on audience relevance, trust and content quality rather than follower count.
2. Verify product access, real experience, licensing, advertising disclosure and permitted claims.
3. Prepare localized hook, task demo, shot list and creator-safe talking points.
4. Review draft for health/finance claims, privacy, copyright and false testimonials.
5. Publish tracked variants and compare activated users and cost by creator.
6. Archive rights, approvals, payout terms and performance.

## Output

Complete the [distribution experiment worksheet](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md), linking primary sources and actual outcomes. Label unexecuted tests not attempted.

## Safeguards

Respect privacy, advertising disclosures, content rights, restricted sectors and local regulatory review. No spam or invented metrics.
