# Pricing and willingness-to-pay research

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Full procedure](../../markets/operations/pricing-and-willingness-to-pay.md)

## Inputs

Specific buyer and market, product version, legal entity, financial assumptions, primary sources, test owner and privacy-safe evidence.

## Procedure

1. Interview current alternatives and budgets without anchoring with your preferred price.
2. Test a truthful offer with explicit price, terms and consent.
3. Record completed purchases or signed pilots separately from stated intent.
4. Model discounts, VAT/sales tax applicability, refunds and payment fees.
5. Review willingness to pay by segment, buyer and job rather than country average.
6. Revisit pricing after retained usage and support cost are observed.

## Output

A source-linked decision record with unknowns, observed results, owner, stop conditions and next review. Mark unrun research as not attempted.

## Guardrails

Pricing experiments must not use deceptive scarcity, hidden fees or unauthorized card charges. No invented financial metrics, unauthorized user data or unsupported legal conclusions.
