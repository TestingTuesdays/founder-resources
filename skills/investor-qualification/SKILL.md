# Investor qualification and Kenya deal evidence

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Detailed guide](../../growth/investor-qualification.md)

## Inputs

Founder stage, target users, actual product, jurisdiction, authorized data, current primary sources and named owner.

## Procedure

1. Collect official fund website and portfolio URL with reviewed date.
2. Record investor type, stage, ticket, sectors, Kenya evidence and current fund status.
3. Find named Kenya portfolio company and dated primary announcement when available.
4. Mark open-to-new-investments and ticket size as unknown unless verified.
5. Score mandate fit, traction fit, geographic fit, intro path and competing portfolio exposure.
6. Update status after outreach and recheck sources before claiming active deployment.

## Completion

Deliver evidence-backed investor catalog with qualified/unverified status and next action. Distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, observed metrics and unverified leads. Record next review date and responsible owner.

## Safety

No invented traction, fake testimonials, unauthorized data sharing, guaranteed algorithm reach or unsupported legal/tax advice. Use qualified professional review for securities, tax and entity matters.
