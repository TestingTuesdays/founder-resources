# Country × industry × channel distribution planning

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Distribution hub](../../distribution/README.md)

## Inputs

Product, buyer segment, legal entity, target country, industry, budget, available channels, primary sources and accountable owner.

## Procedure

1. Select [country](../../distribution/countries/README.md), [industry](../../distribution/industries/README.md) and [channel](../../distribution/channels/README.md).
2. Write one customer problem and local-language positioning statement, validated with target users.
3. Check payment merchant eligibility, advertising rules, privacy, taxes and regulated-sector claims.
4. Produce one original channel-specific content asset and a matched landing page.
5. Define qualified visit, activation, retention and CAC with explicit denominators.
6. Run a capped pilot, collect feedback, and compare observed outcomes against predefined stop criteria.
7. Record source dates, unknowns, actual results and a next decision.

## Deliverable

A completed [experiment template](../../templates/distribution-experiment.md) and source log. Unrun tests remain not attempted.

## Safety

No fabricated market statistics, guaranteed algorithm performance, misleading health/finance claims, spam or nonconsensual tracking.
