# Marketplace dispute and seller quality audit

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Guide](../../docs/marketplace-dispute-operations.md)

## Inputs

Versioned product, real target user task, synthetic test accounts, authorized environment and named reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Define dispute types, evidence, moderation authority and appeal.
2. Test fake listing, non-delivery, refund and chargeback with fictional accounts.
3. Verify seller and buyer data isolation.
4. Test timeouts, duplicate payouts and frozen funds.
5. Check accessible status communication and safe escalation.
6. Measure resolution outcomes and policy consistency.

## Completion criteria

Evidence-backed issue register with observed outcomes, severity, owner, fix, build and retest. Separate hypotheses and proposed tests from executed tests.

## Privacy and safety

Never publish participant identities, payment details, credentials or unapproved security findings. Escalate regulated or safety-critical questions for expert review.
