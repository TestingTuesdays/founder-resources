# Market profitability evaluation

[← Skills](../README.md) · [Market framework](../../markets/profitability-framework.md)

## Inputs

Product, specific buyer segment, candidate countries, business entity, price and cost model, actual acquisition/retention data, official regulatory sources and decision owner.

## Procedure

1. Define a real paid value event and a matching reporting period.
2. Identify three to five plausible countries and one comparable segment per country.
3. Model collected revenue, variable costs, payment/FX, refunds, CAC, retention and local fixed costs.
4. Mark each input observed, quoted, forecast or unknown; never invent a national average.
5. Verify merchant onboarding, legal structure, tax, data and sector feasibility with qualified local review.
6. Run capped paid pilots where lawful and record actual cohort economics.
7. Compare conservative/base/optimistic contribution and payback with explicit assumptions.
8. Recommend one pilot market, document uncertainty and schedule a decision after retention data arrives.

## Output

[Completed scorecard](../../templates/market-profitability-scorecard.md), source log, risk register and go/no-go decision. No country is labeled universally most profitable.

## Safety

No unsupported tax or investment advice, fake CAC, unverified market rankings or use of private customer data in public issues.
