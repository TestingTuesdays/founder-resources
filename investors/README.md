## Confirmed primary-source examples

- [Novastar's official portfolio](https://www.novastarventures.com/portfolio/) includes Kenya-tagged companies such as **BasiGo** (mobility), **Penda Healthcare** (health), **iProcure** (agriculture) and others. This verifies portfolio exposure, not current investment availability.
- [Norrsken22's official site](https://www.norrsken22.com/) describes a growth-stage pan-African technology mandate and a Kenya-based team. A specific Kenya deal needs separate confirmation.
- [FSD Africa Investments](https://fsdafrica.org/fsdai-investments/our-investments/) lists an investment in Kenya-focused **FrontEnd Ventures**, which is a fund investment, not proof of a direct startup investment.

## Critical distinctions

**VC vs angel vs DFI vs accelerator:** different ticket sizes, decision processes, return profiles, instruments and eligibility. **Delaware incorporation:** may be acceptable to some investors but does not establish Kenyan tax or regulatory status. **Portfolio evidence:** confirms historical exposure, not open deployment. **Sector fit:** must be checked against the current fund, not only the firm's brand.

**Current research status:** the catalog intentionally includes unverified leads to support sourcing; do not treat those as confirmed active investors. Check each official portfolio and latest fund announcement before outreach.
