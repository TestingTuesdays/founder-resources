# Sector-specific Kenya investor research map

[← Investors](README.md) · [Catalog](catalog.csv)

This map is **discovery guidance**. It is not a claim that every listed firm actively invests in every sector today. Validate the specific fund's current strategy and portfolio before outreach.

| Startup sector | Investors / capital types to investigate | Evidence and qualification task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fintech and payments | TLcom, 4DX, FSD Africa Investments, Norrsken22, Safaricom Spark | Confirm fintech mandate, CBK considerations and current fund |
| Healthtech and insurtech | Novastar, Villgro Africa, impact investors | Verify Kenya portfolio and clinical/data regulation fit |
| Agritech and food systems | Novastar, DOB Equity, Acumen, Kenya Climate Ventures | Verify value-chain thesis and check sizes |
| Climate, clean energy, carbon | Novastar, Catalyst Fund, Kenya Climate Ventures, Acumen | Distinguish venture equity, grants, project finance and carbon credit instruments |
| Mobility and logistics | Novastar, Norrsken22, corporate strategics | Check hardware/capex appetite and asset-finance mix |
| Enterprise SaaS and AI | Antler, 4DX, Enza, TLcom | Confirm local traction and global-scale thesis |
| Education and workforce | Novastar, impact funds, accelerators | Confirm business model and education portfolio evidence |
| Consumer and commerce | Novastar, DOB Equity, 4DX | Verify unit economics and current mandate |
| Hardware, IoT, utilities | Climate funds, impact investors, corporate ventures | Separate prototype grants from capex and growth equity |
| Govtech and civic | Impact capital, public procurement, accelerators | Government contracting risk and funding instrument |
| Creative/media and gaming | Angel networks, sector funds, accelerators | Avoid asserting a VC mandate without published deals |

A sector is **not** a substitute for investor qualification. See [the methodology](qualification-method.md).
