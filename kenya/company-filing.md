## Filing workflow

1. Choose local private company, foreign-company branch or another appropriate structure with counsel.
2. Check [BRS](https://brs.go.ke/) and [KenInvest eProcedures](https://eprocedures.investkenya.go.ke/) for current filing route and forms.
3. Prepare ownership, directors, registered office, identity and beneficial ownership documents as applicable.
4. Submit through official BRS/eCitizen, retain receipt and registration documents privately.
5. Confirm KRA PIN and actual tax obligations, employer contributions, county permits and sector licenses.
6. Maintain annual-return and ownership-update responsibilities with a company secretary.

| Ongoing item | Authority | Applicability and due date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual returns | BRS | Verify for entity |
| Beneficial ownership changes | BRS | Verify triggers and thresholds |
| Corporate tax and VAT/eTIMS | KRA | Verify activities and registration |
| Payroll and statutory contributions | Relevant authorities | Verify employment |
| County business permit | County government | Verify location/activity |
| Sector approvals | Sector regulator | Verify business model |

[KenInvest official registration guidance](https://eprocedures.investkenya.go.ke/objective/12) includes local and foreign-company paths. Some legacy official pages contain outdated agency or employer-insurance terminology; check current authority guidance. [BRS beneficial ownership manual](https://brs.ecitizen.go.ke/downloads/bo-manual-v1.pdf) explains filing mechanics. Do not rely on this guide for exact rates or deadlines; obtain current professional review.
