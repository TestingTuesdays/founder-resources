## Neutral questions

- Tell me about the last time you needed to solve this problem.
- What did you use instead? How much time and money did it cost?
- Who decides whether to purchase a solution, and who approves payment?
- What prevents you from using current alternatives?
- How do you normally discover and evaluate suppliers?
- What evidence, local support or integration would you require?
- Can you walk through the task in this fictional/sandbox product without guidance?

## Record

Observed task success, existing paid alternative, actual budget/procurement evidence, objections, language/device context, permission to follow up, and explicit unknowns. **Do not** convert a positive interview into a claimed purchase or statistical market share.
