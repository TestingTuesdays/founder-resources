# Skill title

## Objective
The concrete problem and intended audience.

## Required inputs and permissions
Safe sample data, authorized environment, tools, accessibility accommodations and consent where relevant.

## Procedure
1. Action with observable outcome.
2. Edge/error case.
3. Evidence capture (redacted).
4. Verification and retest.

## Done when
Explicit pass/fail criteria and resulting report/artifact.

## Failure and safety handling
Stop conditions, private data restrictions, independent review and escalation path.

## References
Official standards and sources, each reviewed with date.

Do not designate a workflow successful because an AI assistant wrote or suggested it. Actual execution evidence is required.
