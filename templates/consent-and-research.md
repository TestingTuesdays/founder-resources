# Research consent and privacy planning template

This is a draft organizer checklist, **not legal advice or a substitute for approved consent wording**.

- **Purpose and task:** What exactly is being evaluated?
- **Participant criteria and accommodation:** Required experience, device and access needs, without collecting diagnoses unnecessarily.
- **Voluntary participation:** Can stop at any time; do not make marketing opt-in mandatory.
- **Recordings:** Separate and explicit yes/no consent, or use note-only approach.
- **Data collected:** Minimize identity and contact details, use pseudonyms in reports.
- **Storage owner and access:** Who can see raw research?
- **Retention period and deletion route:** State actual practices, not aspirations.
- **Compensation:** Explain without pressuring positive responses.
- **Withdrawal and correction route:** Who handles requests?
- **Cross-border vendors:** Check the actual processing terms before uploading data.

Never publish identifiable testing reports or recordings in a public repository.
