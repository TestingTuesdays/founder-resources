# Which startup credit should you apply for first?

[← Perks](README.md) · [Nairobi shortlist](kenya-nairobi.md) · [Private tracker template](../templates/perk-tracker.md)

This is a **decision guide**, not a promise of approval.

## 1. What legal entity and business model do you have?

**Kenya-incorporated:** Check whether the provider explicitly supports Kenyan legal entities, account verification, cards and settlement. Do not infer this from support for users in Kenya.

**US/other entity:** Verify the company is legitimately formed, own KYC is correct, and provider supports its jurisdiction. Incorporation, accounting and tax may cost more than a perk.

**No legal entity:** Prefer learning resources, trials and startup programs that accept your stage; do not invent a company name or registration.

## 2. Are you bootstrapped, externally funded or partner-backed?

- **Bootstrapped:** inspect genuine direct-application starter tiers (e.g., AWS/Google when your company qualifies). Render's advertised entry tier **requires at least $25k seed funding**, so do not label it bootstrapped.
- **Funded with a qualified investor:** inspect OpenAI VC partner API benefits, Render tiered credits and GitHub for Startups. Ask investor/accelerator for their real authorized route.
- **AI voice product with fewer than 25 people:** ElevenLabs grant may be relevant if commercial and other terms fit.
- **Kenya idea-stage founder:** look at Hazina's published 2026 application window and other regional incubation programs, not only cloud credits.

## 3. Will credits offset actual spending?

Estimate per month: compute, storage, model tokens, bandwidth, team seats and support. Calculate eligible usage *within* the award term, then forecast recurring costs after expiry. Never treat a $100k maximum as cash.

## 4. Common disqualifiers

Already received credits, unsupported country, no incorporated entity, services not included, new-customer requirement, missing investor code, wrong funding stage, exceeding workforce threshold, projects excluded by policy or submission after a deadline.

## 5. Before activation

Use [the private perk tracker](../templates/perk-tracker.md); record approval evidence, billing owner, credit expiration, auto-upgrade behavior, budget alerts and anticipated full-price usage. No API keys, private invoices or personal applicant details in GitHub issues.
