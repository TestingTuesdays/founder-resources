## Qualification
Check published terms, legal entity country, funding, prior usage, expiry, seats, covered features, post-credit bill and data handling. Ask for a small commercial pilot only where public benefits do not fit. For user research, verify participant geography and incentives separately. Do not stack duplicate cloud credits or claim OSS benefits for proprietary apps. Record official evidence and review date before changing status.
