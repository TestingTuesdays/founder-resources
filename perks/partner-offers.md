# Partner-linked startup perks — read the restrictions

[← Perks](README.md) · [All applications](application-playbook.md)

Startup marketplaces are **directories of separate providers**, not a combined balance transferable across services. A benefit visible in a provider's catalog may require active membership, a specific new account or special referral code.

## Example: Render partner offer marketplace

[Render Startup Offers](https://render.com/startups/offers) currently lists third-party opportunities such as Auth0 startup access, Sentry credits, Notion, ElevenLabs, Vanta, Deel and Mintlify. This **does not mean all applicants qualify** or that the listed partner benefit is independent of the Render program. [Render's own startup credits](https://render.com/startups) are distinct and funded at specific tiers with separate eligibility requirements.

## Keep partner benefits separate

| Field | Record before applying |
| --- | --- |
| Provider and source | Original offer page, not just a marketplace logo |
| Unlock requirement | Approved startup, VC code, portfolio membership, new account |
| Existing-user restrictions | Whether existing paid accounts are excluded |
| Cash versus credits | Credit only covers stated products and term |
| Expiry | Date of award, last usable day, downgrade/auto-billing |
| Region | Kenyan incorporated company acceptance vs merely serving Kenyan customers |
| Stacking | Whether credits from other programs conflict |

## Founder warnings

- Do not sum the advertised maximum of every listed partner and call it guaranteed funding.
- Do not enter private referral codes into a public GitHub issue.
- Verify partner provider's own terms before creating paid subscriptions.
- Keep partner discounts as **separate offers**, not duplicate entries inflating the catalog.

_Source: official [Render partner offers](https://render.com/startups/offers), reviewed November 23, 2026._
