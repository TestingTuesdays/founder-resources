# Kenya & Nairobi startup perks — eligibility-first shortlist

[← All perks](README.md) · [Application guide](application-playbook.md)

This shortlist is **relevant to founders operating in Nairobi**, not proof that every Kenya-incorporated business is approved. Confirm supported billing country, KYC, funding, partner membership, startup age and cohort status directly with the provider.

## Self-funded founders

| Program | Why to inspect | Important gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/) | Self-funded Founders path advertises $1k initially, with up to $5k for select companies | New to credits; company website; age and AWS account requirements |
| [Google Cloud for Startups](https://cloud.google.com/startup) | $2k Start tier for some unfunded startups; larger funded tiers | MVP, business model, age, geography and past credit review |
| [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) | Advertises $10k bootstrapped tier | Incorporation, website, business email and other criteria |
| [Microsoft for Startups](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/overview) | Startup credit path with staged growth | Eligible for-profit software product and supported Azure jurisdiction |
| [Notion for Startups](https://www.notion.com/startups) | 1–6 months of paid workspace depending on affiliation | New non-paying customer, company/team eligibility |
| [NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) | Training, tools, partners and AI startup network | Incorporated AI startup, program eligibility and partner benefits vary |

## Partner-backed and funded founders

- [AWS Activate Portfolio](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/) — requires eligible Activate Provider organizational ID, potentially larger credit tier.
- [Google Cloud funded tiers](https://cloud.google.com/startup) — verifiable funding and other criteria; large credit caps are not guaranteed.
- [GitHub for Startups](https://github.com/enterprise/startups) — partner affiliation **and outside funding** needed; GitHub Enterprise account requirements apply.
- [DigitalOcean Hatch](https://try.digitalocean.com/hatch/) — provider states 12-month credits with amount determined by partner route.
- [MongoDB for Startups](https://www.mongodb.com/solutions/startups) — up to $15k in Atlas credits, amount depends on eligibility/stage.

## Nairobi-based programs and real-world support

- [Antler East Africa / Kenya](https://www.antler.co/location/kenya) — founder residency in Nairobi, workspace, mentoring, possible investment and bundled tech credits. Highly selective; funding has equity terms. Confirm cohort dates and whether benefits are available before investment.
- [Google for Startups Accelerator Africa](https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/africa/) — equity-free growth-stage accelerator, mentoring and conditional cloud benefits. **2026 cohort application period closed March 18, 2026**; watch future cohorts.
- [Kenya Climate Innovation Center](https://www.kenyacic.org/) — climate, agriculture and related sector incubator opportunities; **current call and terms not verified**.
- [iHub](https://ihub.co.ke/) — Nairobi ecosystem and programs; **do not assume it grants startup credits** without a specific current offer.
- [Founder Institute](https://fi.co/) — Nairobi/local cohorts when offered, with program-specific fees and terms; check current availability.

## Practical founder checklist

- [ ] Entity country, incorporation date, funding stage, business domain/email and product description ready.
- [ ] Startup is not an excluded consulting, crypto-mining, nonprofit or regulated-category business for the specific offer.
- [ ] Provider supports Kenya billing and verification **for my company**, not just Kenyan users.
- [ ] Founding team has not consumed overlapping or disqualifying credits.
- [ ] No equity, incorporation, bank minimum, annual commitment or hidden payment obligation mistaken for a free grant.
- [ ] Record award amount, covered services, currency, expiration, renewal and post-credit recurring spend privately.
