# Founder perks research and partnership playbook

## Purpose
Web3Clubs / Testing Tuesdays Nairobi: lower founders' build, test and launch costs. Public vendor offers are not signed partnerships. Research date: 2026-11-25. No outreach authorized by this document.

## Research and qualification workflow
1. Discover via official startup programs, accelerator partner pages, https://www.startupcreds.com/programs and https://coloradostartups.org/guides/startup-credits/ (directories are leads only).
2. Record provider, category, official application URL, source date, benefit tier and currency, duration, company stage, incorporation/funding requirements, Kenya eligibility, previous-customer restrictions, payment card, overage/renewal, partner route, owner and next action. Unknown means unknown.
3. Score each 0–2 on founder demand, Kenya accessibility, real savings, evidence quality and ease of redemption; prioritize 8–10/10, but block publication if eligibility or terms are unverified.
4. Separate public self-service, partner prospect, outreach approved, contacted, negotiating, written approval, redemption tested, paused and expired. Seek Foundation approval before outreach; never publish private codes or personal contacts.
5. Pilot with up to five opt-in teams; track eligible referrals, approvals, redemptions, useful usage, realized savings and expiry. Recheck terms monthly; remove expired offers. No sponsor influence on admissions or scoring.
6. GitHub public directory holds sources and methodology; Notion holds operational pipeline, contacts and private correspondence. No private data in public commits.
