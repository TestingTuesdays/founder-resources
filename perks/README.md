A curated directory of cloud credits, developer tools, startup software discounts, accelerator and mentoring support. **A listed program is not a promise of acceptance or an endorsement.** Country availability, stage, funding, partner access, exclusions and expiration all matter.

**Evidence labels:** **Official terms reviewed** = provider program page reviewed on 2026-11-23 (still verify before applying). **Program discovery — recheck** = relevant provider/program, but amount, current opening or details not confirmed. **Lead — offer not verified** = founder research lead, *not* confirmation that a startup-specific discount exists. **Official program discovery** = broad ecosystem access, not a separate award. Data source: [catalog.csv](catalog.csv).

## Best first applications for a Nairobi founder

If your company is incorporated and the service is offered to your entity, start with [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/), [Google Cloud Start](https://cloud.google.com/startup), [Microsoft for Startups](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/overview), [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) and [NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) for a relevant AI startup. Funded/partner-backed teams can separately examine GitHub for Startups and larger credit tiers. See [country-specific caveats](kenya-nairobi.md).

## Critical founder warnings

1. **Maximum credits are not your award.** Most headline values are capped, tiered or available only through eligible partners and investors.
2. **Credits are not cash.** They usually apply only to certain services, accounts and fixed periods. A credit may not cover tax, support, marketplace charges or every AI model.
3. **Track expiration and autopay.** Set billing budgets and alerts; calculate how much real spend a credit offsets and the post-credit bill.
4. **Check Kenya support.** A program saying “global” does not guarantee a Kenyan legal entity, payment card, company verification or all cloud regions are accepted.
5. **Do not create a legal entity just for perks.** Stripe Atlas, banking and investor programs have real fees, tax implications, KYC and possible equity obligations.
6. **Avoid duplicate credit applications and stacking claims.** Check whether past grants, partner benefits or accounts make you ineligible.
7. **No pay-to-play placement.** Every submission needs a provider source, full terms and a review date. Update outdated entries rather than posting referral codes.
