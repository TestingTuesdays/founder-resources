# Kenya founder support beyond cloud credits

[← Perks directory](README.md) · [Nairobi shortlist](kenya-nairobi.md)

Not all valuable founder resources are software coupons. Nairobi founders can also benefit from mentorship, testing users, incubation, sector guidance, community, and introductions. These listings are for **discovery**: do not interpret inclusion as a live open application, award, formal Testing Tuesdays partnership, or guaranteed investment.

## Before you claim a perk

1. Confirm your actual company/entity is eligible, including Kenya incorporation and business address.
2. Distinguish mentorship, a program application, a **software credit**, a grant and an **equity investment**.
3. Search for a current official call, deadline and participant requirements.
4. Compare value with expected time, equity, fees, office attendance and future billing.
5. Keep private applications, identity checks and financial records out of public GitHub issues.

If a current verified member-only offer is negotiated, publish signed/approved terms separately; never imply an ecosystem organization is a confirmed Testing Tuesdays sponsor.
